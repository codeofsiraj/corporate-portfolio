Frequent Questions

B2B & OEM SUPPLY FAQ

What are Autofocus's Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) for custom assemblies?

For standard catalog components, MOQs start at 500 units per order batch. For custom engineered OEM assemblies requiring new tooling setup, typical initial production runs start at 2,500 units, depending on component complexity.

How does Autofocus support Just-In-Time (JIT) supply chain integration?

We connect directly to your plant's ERP system via EDI. Safety stock buffer inventory is maintained at our nearest regional distribution hub, enabling guaranteed 24-to-48-hour delivery windows directly to your factory line.

What quality certifications do your manufacturing plants hold?

All Autofocus primary manufacturing plants hold current ISO 9001:2015, IATF 16949:2016 automotive quality management certifications, and ISO 14001 environmental management accreditations.

What is the typical lead time for custom engineering prototypes?

Rapid 3D CAD modeling and initial CNC prototype samples are delivered within 10 to 14 business days. Full metallurgical stress testing reports and PPAP Level 3 documentation are delivered within 4 weeks.
